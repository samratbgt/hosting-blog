A2 Hosting has built a reputation as one of the fastest shared hosting providers in the industry. Their "Turbo" servers promise up to 20x faster page loads compared to standard hosting, making them a popular choice for performance-conscious website owners.

With plans starting at just $2.99/month and innovative offerings like AI Sitebuilder and Ultrafast VPS, A2 Hosting provides diverse solutions for different needs. We've been testing their services extensively to give you the complete picture, covering their performance, features, and whether the speed claims hold up under real-world conditions.

A2 Hosting's performance results were impressive:
- **Average Load Time**: 1.2 seconds
- **Uptime**: 99.91% (solid reliability)
- **Time to First Byte**: 320ms
- **PageSpeed Score**: 93/100

### Turbo Server Technology
A2 Hosting's Turbo servers include:
- **LiteSpeed Web Server**: Faster than Apache and Nginx

- **HTTP/2**: Modern protocol for faster loading
- **SSD Storage**: All plans include SSD drives
