Bluehost has been one of the most recognizable names in web hosting for over two decades. As an officially recommended WordPress hosting provider, it's often the first choice for beginners looking to start their website journey. But does Bluehost live up to its reputation in 2025?

After extensive testing over the past 6 months, we've put together this comprehensive review covering everything from performance and features to pricing and support quality.

## Key Features & Performance

### WordPress Optimized Hosting
Bluehost offers specialized WordPress hosting with one-click installation, automatic updates, and WordPress-specific support. Their servers are optimized for WordPress performance, and they provide staging environments for testing changes.

### Performance Results
During our testing period, we monitored several key performance metrics:

- **Average Load Time**: 1.8 seconds
- **Uptime**: 99.7% (industry average: 99.5%)
- **Time to First Byte**: 420ms
- **PageSpeed Score**: 87/100

While not the fastest hosting provider we've tested, Bluehost delivers solid performance that's more than adequate for most websites.

### Security Features
Bluehost includes several security features in their hosting plans:
- Free SSL certificates
- SiteLock security monitoring
- CodeGuard backup service
- Spam protection
- Hotlink protection
